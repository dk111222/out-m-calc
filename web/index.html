Questions parents ask

Does StepMath need an internet connection? +

No. After the app is installed, lessons, speed drills, progress, and the error bank work offline. A connection is used only to download the app and to complete a purchase in the App Store or Google Play.

Can I turn the timer off? +

Yes. A speed drill can end on a timer or after a set number of problems, and you can pause a timed round. Lessons, where a child fills in each step, are not timed.

How does it work with more than one child? +

Add a profile for each child. Each profile keeps its own grade, progress, and error bank. One child's missed problems are not mixed into another child's review. The free version includes one child. The one-time purchase adds more profiles.

Are there ads? +

No. There are no ads in lessons or drills, and a child never has to watch an ad to continue.

Do you collect children's data? +

No. A child's name, grade, answers, and scores stay on the device. StepMath does not upload student data and does not sell or share personal information. A child does not get an account. Wrong-problem practice asks the parent to sign in on the device; that email and password stay there. You can delete one profile or erase all local data in the app. The privacy policy has the details.

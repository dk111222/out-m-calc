Privacy Policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

The short version

  • Practice stays on the phone or tablet you own.
  • We do not upload student names, answers, or scores.
  • We do not show ads or build an advertising profile of a child.
  • You can delete a child's profile, or erase all local data, in the app.

Who this covers

This policy covers the StepMath app for iOS and Android, and this website. StepMath is an offline mental math app for grades K–5. A parent or teacher sets it up. A child uses it to practice.

Lessons and speed drills do not require an account. There is no child login and no class cloud. Wrong-problem practice asks the parent to sign in on the device. That email and password are stored locally and are not uploaded.

Information stored on your device

If you add a learner, the app saves that information in local storage on the device:

  • The name and grade you enter for each child
  • Which trick or drill you assigned
  • Answers, scores, missed problems, and mastery on that device
  • Settings, such as whether a drill uses a timer

That information is not uploaded to StepMath. We do not operate a server that receives student records. Uninstalling the app, deleting a profile, or using Erase all data removes it from the device.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, or the device microphone for practice.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not share student work with advertisers, schools we do not know, or data brokers.
  • We do not use a third-party analytics SDK to profile how a child practices.
  • The app does not show third-party ads.

Children's privacy and COPPA

The Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) applies to operators of online services directed to children under 13 that collect personal information from those children. StepMath is built so that a parent or teacher is the person who sets up the app, and so that practice data is not collected online.

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children over the internet.
  • A child does not create an account, type an email address, or chat with anyone inside the app.
  • Names and grades that an adult types stay in local storage. They are not transmitted to us.
  • We do not use children's information for behavioral advertising, profiling, or sale.
  • A parent or teacher can delete one learner, or erase all local data, without emailing us. Erasing data asks for the adult's 4-digit code. A child in practice mode cannot erase the device data.

This website is written for adults. It does not ask a child to submit a name, a photo, or an email address. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it from our inbox.

GDPR and UK GDPR

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, this is how the roles fall.

Practice records never leave your device, so StepMath does not receive them and does not act as a controller or a processor of a student database. You, the parent or teacher, decide what is stored on the device you control. You can review it in the app and delete it there.

If you email us, we are the controller of that message. We use it only to reply, or to handle the request you made, such as a privacy question. The legal basis is the request you sent us (steps taken at your request before a contract, or our legitimate interest in answering you). We keep the thread only as long as we need it to finish that conversation, then delete it. We do not use it to market to a child.

Because we do not hold student records, we do not transfer them outside your device, and we do not make an automated decision about a child. Where a privacy law gives you a right of access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection, use the in-app delete controls for data on the device. For an email you sent us, contact the address below and we will honor the request. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. We would like the chance to help first.

Purchases

The full library is a one-time purchase. Apple or Google processes the payment. StepMath does not receive your card number. Their privacy policy covers the payment. The store may tell the app that the purchase is active so the unlock can turn on. That receipt is not a student record.

This website

The site is a set of static pages. It has no account, no form, and no analytics cookie. The typeface and the stylesheet are files on this site. Opening a page does not create a profile.

The email link opens your own mail app. We receive only what you choose to write and send. Store buttons leave this site for Apple or Google.

How to delete information

  • In the app, delete one learner to remove that child's name, progress, and error bank from the device.
  • Use Erase all data to clear every learner and return to the start. This asks for the adult's 4-digit code.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its local storage from the device, subject to your phone's backup settings. If you do not want a backup to keep the data, turn off backup for StepMath in the device settings before you delete it.
  • To delete an email you sent us, write to the address below and ask.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. If a future version of the app starts uploading student data, we will say so here before that version does it, and we will not do it in the background of an install that was offline-only.

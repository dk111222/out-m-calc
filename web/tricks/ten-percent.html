10%

Divide the amount by 10.

5% is half of 10%.

10% = ÷ 10 80 ÷ 10 = 8

10% of $80 is $8.

  1. 1
    What to divide by

    10% is one tenth, so divide by 10.

  2. 2
    Find that part

    80 ÷ 10 = 8. That part is $8.

10% of $120

  1. 10% means divide by 10.

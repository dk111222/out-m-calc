If the digits add up to less than 10, put the sum in the middle.

48 × 11 needs a carry. It is 528.

3 8 5

Keep 3 and 5 on the outside. Write 3 + 5 in the middle.

  1. 1
    Add the digits

    3 + 5 = 8.

  2. 2
    Put the sum in the middle

    Write 3, 8, 5. That is 385.
